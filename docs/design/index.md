# Robot System Design

This section documents the development of a low-cost
suction-based window-cleaning robot designed to operate
on vertical glass surfaces.

## Engineering Subsystems

### Mechanical Design

Development of the lightweight 3D-printed chassis,
wheel arrangement and locomotion system.

[View Mechanical Design](/design/mechanical)

### Electrical Design

Design of the robot's electrical architecture,
including the power supply, motor drivers,
controller and sensors.

[View Electrical Design](/design/electrical)

### Suction Adhesion System

Development of the negative-pressure adhesion
mechanism used to maintain attachment to glass.

[View Suction System](/design/suction)

## Preliminary Design Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Dimensions | 200 × 200 × 80 mm |
| Target Mass | 1.5 kg |
| Chassis Material | PLA |
| Manufacturing | 3D Printing |
| Adhesion | Single suction chamber |
| Locomotion | Motor-driven wheels |

::: info Development Status
The robot is undergoing mechanical optimisation
and subsystem development.
:::
